## Why a simulated panel at all

No real advertiser knows the true ROI of its own media plan. That is the difficulty
the field is organised around, and it means "did the model recover the truth?"
cannot be asked of real data. It is asked here instead, of a panel built from
`config/dgp.yaml`, committed before any model was fitted.

**Average ROI is not marginal ROI, and which is larger is not fixed.** A Hill curve
with a slope above one is S-shaped: below its inflection the response is convex,
marginal exceeds average, and the channel is under-invested. Above it the curve is
concave and marginal falls below average. Both regimes are present here, which is
what makes the allocation in `reports/triangulation.md` a decision rather than a
ranking.

## The deliberate misspecification

- **Generated with**: high collinearity, Delayed geometric: w_l proportional to alpha**((l - theta)**2), normalised, which peaks at lag theta, Hill saturation.
- **Fitted with**: Geometric adstock and logistic saturation. Geometric adstock is the generating kernel with theta pinned to zero, so it cannot represent a delayed peak at all, and a logistic curve cannot take the Hill shape. The mismatch is deliberate: a model fitted to data its own functional form produced recovers its own assumptions and measures nothing. The matched arm of the recovery grid separates misspecification error from identification error.

A practical note that shaped the design. The kernel was originally a Weibull PDF.
`WeibullPDFAdstock` raises `TypeError: x must be have an XTensorType` under
pymc-marketing 0.19.2 with pytensor 2.38.2, for every saturation, so the matched arm
could not be fitted at all. `WeibullCDFAdstock` samples cleanly but its kernel is a
survival curve and decays monotonically, so it cannot represent a delayed peak
either. The delayed-geometric form both admits a delayed peak and can be fitted,
which is what makes the matched arm genuinely matched rather than merely close.

The detrended share is the one that bounds identification. The raw share is dominated by Olist's fivefold growth across the window, which any media-mix model absorbs into its own trend and seasonality terms rather than having to explain with media.

## Attribution, and its one pre-registered knob

Last-click here is a parametric caricature, not a simulated journey: tracking_rate is the share of a channel's true contribution it observes, and organic_capture is the share of baseline revenue it credits to that channel. No claim is made about attribution mechanics, only about the consequences of a stated bias.

search_nonbrand carries tracking_rate 1.0 and organic_capture 0.0, so last-click recovers its true ROI exactly. A harness that only ever showed attribution failing would have had its answer chosen for it.
