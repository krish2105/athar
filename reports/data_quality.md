## The window

`2017-01-02` to `2018-08-13` — **85 weeks**, none of them empty.

Starts the week after the last week with zero orders, which removes the nine-week collection gap in late 2016. Ends at the last week carrying at least 70% of the median of the 8 weeks before it, which removes the mid-week truncation of the extract. The comparison is against recent weeks rather than the whole series because Olist grew throughout, so a half-collected final week can still sit above the global median.

Revenue is sum of order_items.price, excluding freight, recognised at order_purchase_timestamp. currency is brl, 2017-2018. Cancelled and unavailable orders
are excluded; in-flight ones are kept, because an order that is merely invoiced at
the end of the extract is a real purchase a real campaign could have caused, and
dropping it would bias the most recent weeks downward precisely where the series is
thinnest.

## Verification

`athar.frame` stages its join through CTEs; the check in `scripts/build_frame.py`
uses a flat join and filter and shares no code path. They agree on the order count
exactly and on revenue to 1.03e-12
relative — 1.38e-05 BRL on 1.3e7.

They cannot agree bit-for-bit and it would be wrong to require it. Float addition is
not associative, the two query plans accumulate 96,731 terms in
different orders, and DuckDB is already pinned to a single thread. So the test is
agreement to the centavo — the resolution the quantity actually has — with the raw
difference recorded so a reader can see how far inside tolerance it sits.

| Check | Result |
|---|---:|
| Independent order count matches | True |
| Revenue difference (BRL) | 1.38e-05 |
| Tolerance (BRL) | 0.01 |
| Zero-order weeks inside the window | 0 |

## The geography, and the power problem it creates

27 states, a balanced panel of 2,295 state-weeks. One state
carries 38.1% of revenue and
13 of the 27 carry under 1%
each. That concentration is what makes a geo holdout expensive, and it is Olist's
rather than this project's invention — `reports/experiments.md` measures the
consequence.
